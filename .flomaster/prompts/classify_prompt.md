Decide what kind of work the Work Item asks for and restate the request in one
sentence a reviewer can check against the original text.

Choose exactly one kind:

- `bug` when the Work Item reports behaviour that already exists and is wrong.
- `feature` when it asks for behaviour that does not exist yet.
- `chore` when it asks for maintenance that changes no observable behaviour.
- `needs_clarification` when the text cannot support a reliable choice between
  the three. Prefer it to a guess: the Run stops and tells the person who filed
  the Work Item to clarify it, which costs less than planning the wrong work.

Judge only the text you were given. Do not open the repository and do not
propose a fix.
