A reviewer asked for one change before this work can be relied on. Make that change in
the Workspace and report what you did.

Do exactly what was asked and nothing else. A repair that also tidies something nearby
costs the next reviewer the ability to tell the two apart, and the next pass has to judge
the repair alone. If what was asked cannot be done as stated, say so plainly in place of
a resolution rather than doing something adjacent and calling it done.

Report one sentence a reviewer can check against the Workspace: what changed, and where.
Report what you did rather than which concern you were answering, because the concern is
the reviewer's to name and the next pass reads the Workspace rather than your report.
